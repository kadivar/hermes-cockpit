# How a contribution is licensed

**The Maintainer** is Mohammadreza Kadivar, the copyright holder who publishes this project (named in `NOTICE`). **You** are the person who
opens a pull request.

The project is published under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or any later version
(AGPL-3.0-or-later; the text is in `LICENSE`). The Maintainer also offers it under separate commercial licences
(`COMMERCIAL.md`). A change can be part of both only when the Maintainer holds the right to license it both ways, so
a pull request is received in one of two ways, and you choose which.

## 1. As a report (the box is not ticked)

This is what happens when you say nothing. Your pull request is offered under AGPL-3.0-or-later, like any change to
the project, and nothing more is granted.

- It is **not merged**.
- The Maintainer reads it as a report: of a defect, a missing case or an idea.
- It is closed with thanks. The Maintainer may then write a change of their own for the same problem, from the
  problem and not from your code.

Nothing is owed on either side.

## 2. As a contribution to merge (the box is ticked)

By ticking the box in the pull request you state:

> I license this contribution to the Maintainer under the BSD Zero Clause License (0BSD; the text is in
> `LICENSES/0BSD.txt`).

With that grant the Maintainer may publish your contribution as part of the project under AGPL-3.0-or-later and under
any other terms the Maintainer chooses, a commercial licence included.

You retain all right, title and interest in your contribution. You may use it elsewhere as you wish.

By ticking the box you also confirm that:

- you wrote the contribution, or you have the right to license it this way;
- if your employer has rights in what you write, your employer allows this;
- you know of nothing in it that belongs to someone else and is not stated in the pull request.

The Maintainer is not obliged to merge a contribution, and a merged contribution gives no claim to payment, to a share
of any licence fee, or to a say in what becomes of the project.

## The record

The pull request is the record: its description with the box as you left it, and its commits. A box ticked by anyone
but the author of the commits does not count.
