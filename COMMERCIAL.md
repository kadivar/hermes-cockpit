# Licensing

## Open source

Cockpit is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and the copyright notice in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

Anyone may use it under those terms: a person, a team or a company, at work, for any purpose. Nobody needs to ask,
and nothing on this page adds a condition to the licence or takes one away.

One folder is not under that licence: the starter pack under `starter/` is 0BSD (`LICENSES/0BSD.txt`), so that a pack
made from it is yours. `cockpit init` copies those files into your pack, and no condition follows the copy.

## What the licence asks

In short, and as a pointer rather than as legal advice: if you change cockpit and let other people use your changed
version over a network, the licence asks you to offer those people the source of your version under the same licence.
The same holds when you pass a copy on. What it requires in your situation is stated by the licence itself, which is
the only authority: read [the text](LICENSE) and the Free Software Foundation's
[questions and answers](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.html), and ask your own counsel where it matters.

## A commercial licence

A commercial licence is available for an organisation whose policies do not permit software under the AGPL, or that
wants terms other than the AGPL's. It is a separate written agreement with the copyright holder, and its scope is
agreed with you.

## Contributions

A contribution is merged only when its author licenses it to the maintainer under 0BSD, as [CONTRIBUTION-TERMS.md](CONTRIBUTION-TERMS.md) says. A contributor keeps the copyright in
their work and grants the maintainer the right to license it under AGPL-3.0-or-later and under other terms. That
grant exists precisely so that both licences can be offered: the open-source licence to everyone, and a commercial
licence to those who ask for one. Every published version that includes a contribution stays available under
AGPL-3.0-or-later.
